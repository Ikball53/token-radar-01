How the radar reads

Implied value. For every protocol whose token already trades, the snapshot gives a market cap and a TVL, so the TVL-to-market-cap multiple is known. The radar takes the median multiple inside each category (the global median where a category has too few token-bearing protocols), multiplies a pre-token protocol's TVL by it, then adjusts by 7-day TVL growth (clamped) and by age with no token (up to +50% for three years of track record).

Coverage. The board holds the snapshot's whole 40-protocol watchlist plus the 160 highest-TVL remaining protocols that record no token. A protocol is listed as pre-token because its DefiLlama record carries no token — the record is not proof that it has none.

Farm plans. Each row's plan is built from that protocol's own figures: its chain sets the gas cost, its TVL sets the position size, its 7-day change sets the tone. Costs are in USD and every step names a timeframe.

Data: sealed DefiLlama snapshot, fetched once. This page renders with no network; every figure on it comes from that snapshot and this radar's model alone.
