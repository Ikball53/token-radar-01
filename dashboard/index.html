Launch Radar

What would a protocol’s token be worth if its record stayed as it is — and what should a reader do to farm it before it launches one.

How the value is worked out

implied value = TVL × 0.20 × growth × maturity

  • 0.20 is the base multiple: the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the snapshot’s 144 protocols whose token already trades (median 0.2049, rounded to 0.20).
  • growth = 1 + the protocol’s 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50% … +100%. No change reported counts as 1.00.
  • maturity = 0.75 + 0.25 × (years tracked, capped at 4) ÷ 4. A protocol that ran longer with no token counts for more.

Watchlist: protocols that record no token, hold at least $50M of TVL, and are not a centralised exchange, a bridge, a liquid-staking or restaking product, a wrapped or staked product, a versioned deployment, or an exchange-branded product. The snapshot’s own watchlist of 40 sits fully inside it.

An implied value is this board’s own claim. It is not a prediction of whether or when a token launches. A protocol is said to record no token, never that it has none.

Data: DefiLlama protocol snapshot, fetched 14 Nov 2026, sealed for this competition. Every figure on this page is computed live from that snapshot by the code in app.js; nothing is fetched from the network.

Farm steps are concrete actions with the capital, gas and holding time each one needs. Costs are estimates in US dollars at snapshot time.
